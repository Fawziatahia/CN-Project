Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 30, 2026

TalkNest is a lightweight chat app designed to be simple and respectful of your privacy. This page explains what we collect, why we collect it, and what we don't do.

What we collect

  • Display name — the name you choose to chat under. This is the only identifier we ask for.
  • Messages you send — stored so you and the recipient can see your chat history.
  • Connection state — whether you're online, offline, or typing, so the other person sees accurate status.

What we do not collect

  • No email address, phone number, or password.
  • No third-party tracking, advertising, or analytics cookies.
  • No location data, contacts, or device identifiers.

How your data is used

  • To deliver your messages to the person you're chatting with.
  • To show online presence, typing indicators, and read receipts.
  • To keep your chat history available when you log back in.

How your data is stored

Messages and display names are stored in a local SQLite database on the server running TalkNest. Data is not shared with third parties.

Your choices

  • You can stop using TalkNest at any time — just close the tab.
  • If you're running your own TalkNest instance, you control the database and can delete it whenever you want.

Contact

Questions or concerns? Reach out to the maintainer of the TalkNest instance you're using.
